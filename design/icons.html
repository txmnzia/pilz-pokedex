PILZDEX ICON STYLES

Each option is drawn on the real Pilzdex surfaces: filter bar, two grid tiles (one caught, one not), list rows and the species badge. Pick one edibility style (1 to 5) and one caught marker (A to H).

EDIBILITY STYLES

Four states: edible, no interest, toxic, deadly. The grey "no-entry" sign is gone in every option: "no interest" is now an empty plate or a neutral mark. The skull has no red ring.

CAUGHT MARKERS

In the games, the dex list put a small capture-ball icon next to every species you owned, and showed nothing for species only seen. Pilzdex tiles already show your photo when caught, so the marker matters most in the list. Shown here with edibility style 1.

Reply with a number and a letter, for example 2 + B. Mixing is fine too, for example "style 3 but with the skull from 1".
