fungitastic_int8.tflite
FungiTastic DINOv3 ViT-S/16 @ 384 px, 2,827 fungi species, by Paul Bauriegel
https://huggingface.co/paulbauriegel/fungitastic-dinov3-vits16-384
Modified for Pilzdex: mushroom_model_dynamic.tflite quantized to int8 weights (ai-edge-quantizer dynamic_wi8_afp32,
scripts/quantize-ft.py in the Pilzdex repository). No other change.

Weights derive from DINOv3 (Meta), covered by the DINOv3 License: see LICENSE-dinov3.md in this folder.
Training data: FungiTastic (Picek et al., 2024, arXiv:2408.13632), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Used here for a private,
non-commercial app; the conservative reading is that the weights inherit the non-commercial and share-alike terms.
